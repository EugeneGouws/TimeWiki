# Summary: `docs/BRAINSTORM.md`

Origin document of the "minimal-teacher timetabling" brainstorm project —
predates [[timemath-project]]'s current Phase 0–5 structure. Explicit rule
at the time: no building, brainstorming only (later superseded once
PRODUCT.md put implementation in scope — flagged as superseded in the
`solver-docs` copy, see [[solver-docs-consolidation]]). Defines the outcome
sought (teacher pools, marginal-impact leftover distribution, a lower-bound
proof, characterisation of what moves the bound) and the method (start tiny,
hand-verifiable, scale stepwise with O-notation tracked). Established facts
here are the seed of [[bounds-ladder]] (true-group reconstruction, JSON
`m`-field staleness, the measured bound ladder). Open questions here (exact
tight lower bound, greedy near-optimality, basket-diversity effect) persist
unresolved into the current project.
